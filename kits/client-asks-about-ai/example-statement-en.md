### Other AI use

- Staff may not use personal AI accounts for client work.

## What goes into them

- Client material goes in only with names and other identifying details removed.
- Sensitive data, such as citizen service numbers (BSN), health data or other special categories of personal data, never goes into AI tools.

## How we work

- A person checks AI output before anything goes to a client, and whoever signs it off is responsible for it.
- We have written rules for AI tools, shared with all staff.
- For our clients' data we usually act as their processor: we handle it on their behalf, under a processing agreement. An AI tool we use on that data is then a sub-processor.
- If client data goes into an AI tool by mistake, we record it, assess the risk, and tell the client without undue delay where we are their processor.

## Your choices

- If a client prefers that we don't use AI tools on their work, we don't.
