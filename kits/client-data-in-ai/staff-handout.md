# Put company information into an AI tool by mistake?

It happens, and it can be handled. Do these three things, in this order.

**Who to tell in our firm: ______________________ (name, phone)**

## 1. Note it (now, 5 minutes)

Describe what happened. Don't paste the information again anywhere, not even into an email to your manager.

- Which tool, and which account: your personal account, or a work account your firm gave you.
- When, roughly.
- What kind of information, and whose: for example "a client's payroll file with about 40 names and salaries".
- Pasted text, an uploaded file, or a connected mailbox or drive.
- Whether you shared the chat with a link, and whether the tool's training setting was on.

## 2. Tell your firm (today)

Tell the person named above. You can use this message:

Hi [name], I need to report something. On [date] at about [time] I put [describe the kind of information, not the information itself] from [a client / our own files] into [tool], using my [personal / work] account. I have [not yet] deleted the conversation. Can we talk today?

## 3. Delete it (after you've noted it)

Delete the conversation and remove any share link. If the training setting was on, switch it off. Wait first if your firm asks you to: it may want to see the chat for its record.

### Why today?

If personal data was involved, your firm may have to report it to the Dutch data protection authority (the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). The law says: as soon as possible, and within 72 hours of finding out where it can. It must also record every data breach, including the ones it doesn't report. It can only do any of that if it knows.

### If you used a work account

If your firm gave you a work account (for example ChatGPT Business, or Copilot with the green shield) and its rules allow this kind of information in it, this may not be an incident at all. The Dutch data protection authority says that when chatbot use is part of an organisation's policy, it is not a data breach. If you're not sure what the rules allow, tell your manager anyway.
