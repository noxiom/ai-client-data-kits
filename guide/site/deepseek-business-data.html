Questions

Is DeepSeek GDPR compliant?

We don't label any tool compliant or not, but DeepSeek's own app is a poor fit for client data: personal data is stored in China, it is used for training unless you opt out by email, and there is no processor agreement. In February 2025 the Dutch data protection authority warned users to be very careful and announced an investigation into data transfers to China.2, 1

Does DeepSeek have a business plan?

We found none for the app and website: its privacy policy describes no business accounts or processor agreement.2

What about DeepSeek models run by other providers?

DeepSeek's privacy policy says it doesn't cover apps that developers build on its platform, and companies that run DeepSeek's models on their own infrastructure have their own terms. Those can be very different, so assess each one separately.2
