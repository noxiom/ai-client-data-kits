Copilot personal vs work account: what happens to your business data

"Copilot" is three different products. The consumer app with a personal Microsoft account has no contract with your firm. Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, used with a work account, is covered by Microsoft's Data Protection Addendum, shows a green shield and costs nothing extra. The paid Microsoft 365 Copilot licence adds access to your organisation's mail and files. In both work versions, web search queries sit outside the DPA.

How to tell which one someone is using

  • Work account with enterprise data protection: Microsoft shows a green shield at the top of Copilot, next to the New Chat button.
  • No green shield means a personal Microsoft account, under consumer terms.
  • Copilot inside Word or Outlook from a Microsoft 365 Personal or Family subscription is the consumer product too, even on a work laptop.

What an admin should check
  • Make sure people use Copilot signed in with their work account. Ask them to look for the green shield.
  • Decide on web search. Queries sent to Bing aren't covered by the DPA or the EU Data Boundary. The "Allow web search in Copilot" policy switches it off.
  • Stop personal-account sign-in to Copilot on company devices with tenant restrictions. Microsoft advises against simply blocking the Copilot address.
  • Check the Copilot Chat usage report to see who uses it.
  • Before buying Microsoft 365 Copilot licences, review who can open which SharePoint sites, Teams and shared files. Copilot surfaces everything a user can open.

Questions

What does the green shield in Copilot mean?

It shows that enterprise data protection applies: the person is signed in with a work (Entra ID) account, and prompts and responses fall under Microsoft's Data Protection Addendum and Product Terms. No green shield means a personal Microsoft account and consumer terms.3, 4

What's the difference between Copilot Chat and Microsoft 365 Copilot?

Copilot Chat comes with eligible Microsoft 365 work accounts at no extra cost and works from the web plus whatever people paste or upload. Microsoft 365 Copilot is a paid licence that can also draw on your organisation's mail, files, meetings and Teams through Microsoft Graph, limited to what each user is allowed to open.6, 5, 4

Is Microsoft 365 Copilot GDPR compliant?

No tool is compliant on its own; compliance depends on how your firm uses it. Microsoft 365 Copilot gives you what compliant use needs from the vendor: a DPA with Microsoft as processor, no training on your prompts, and EU Data Boundary processing for EU customers. Two things fall outside: web search queries sent to Bing, and Anthropic models, which Microsoft lists as currently excluded from the EU Data Boundary.5, 3

Does the consumer Copilot app train on my conversations?

For the updated app (available since 18 August 2026), Microsoft says prompts, responses and files aren't used to train foundation models. Optional feedback may still be used to improve Copilot. The older app did train on conversations unless you opted out.1, 2
