Gemini personal vs Workspace: what happens to your business data

With a personal Google account, Gemini trains on chats by default, human reviewers read some of them, and reviewed chats are kept for up to three years. With a Google Workspace work account, Google doesn't train on your data without permission, prompts fall under its Cloud Data Processing Addendum, and admins set retention and, on most editions, a European data region.

How to tell which one someone is using

  • Look at the account shown in the corner of Gemini. A company address managed by your Google Workspace means Workspace terms; a personal Gmail address means consumer terms. Google itself notes that work accounts may be subject to different data handling terms.
  • Google AI Pro is a personal Google One subscription. Personal-account terms apply even when someone pays for it to use at work.

What an admin should check
  • Decide whether the Gemini app should be available: Google turns it on by default as a core service.
  • Set how long Gemini conversations are kept. The default for the Gemini app is 18 months.
  • On Business Standard and higher editions, set the data region to Europe if you want data at rest in the EU. Gemini Notebook isn't covered by data regions.
  • Ask staff to use Gemini for work only while signed in with their work account, not a personal Gmail address.

Questions

What's the difference between Gemini with a personal account and with Workspace?

Different terms. Personal accounts: training on by default for adults, human review of some chats, reviewed chats kept up to three years. Workspace accounts: no training without your organisation's permission, no human review outside your domain without permission, prompts covered by the Cloud Data Processing Addendum, and retention set by your admins.1, 2, 3

Does Gemini in Google Workspace train on our data?

Google says Workspace does not use customer data for training models without the customer's prior permission or instruction.3

Can people at Google read Gemini chats?

For personal accounts, yes: human reviewers, including reviewers at Google's service providers, read some chats, and Google asks users not to enter confidential information they wouldn't want a reviewer to see. For Workspace, Google says content isn't reviewed by humans outside your domain without permission.1, 3

Is Google AI Pro a business plan?

No. Google AI Pro is a personal Google One subscription, so personal-account terms apply even if someone pays for it to use at work. Gemini for business comes with Google Workspace.5, 6
