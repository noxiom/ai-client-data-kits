Claude personal vs Team: what happens to your business data

Claude's personal plans (Free, Pro, Max) use chats for training unless each person switches it off, and keep that data for up to five years if they don't. Team and Enterprise don't train on customer content and include Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum. Data on the work plans is stored in the US, and only Enterprise can set a retention limit.

What an admin should check
  • Leave the Development Partner Program off unless you mean to share your data with Anthropic for training.
  • Verify your domain and switch on single sign-on (both available on Team).
  • Team has no custom retention setting. If you need a retention limit, that is an Enterprise feature.
  • Enterprise: use domain capture to bring personal accounts created with your work domain into the organisation.
  • Data is stored in the US. Check that the standard contractual clauses in Anthropic's DPA fit what your client agreements say about transfers.
Client data already went into a personal account?

In a personal account, delete the chat: Anthropic deletes it from back-end storage within 30 days. If the Model Improvement setting was on, de-identified data may already be in training pipelines, where Anthropic says it can be kept for up to five years. Record which it was.6, 1

Questions

What's the difference between Claude's personal plans and Team?

Personal plans (Free, Pro, Max) run on consumer terms: training unless each person switches it off, no DPA, no admin controls. Team runs on Anthropic's Commercial Terms: no training on your content, a DPA included, single sign-on and central billing.2, 4, 3

How long does Claude keep data on the Team plan?

Chats stay in the product until they are deleted. Deleted chats leave Anthropic's back-end storage within 30 days, and content flagged for safety review can be kept for up to 2 years. Custom retention periods are an Enterprise feature; Team doesn't have them.7, 8, 3

Why does Claude mention five years?

That applies to the personal plans. If a user lets Anthropic use their chats to improve Claude, de-identified data may be kept in training pipelines for up to five years. With the setting off, the period is 30 days.1, 6

Is Claude data stored in the EU?

Anthropic says data is stored in the US, while requests may be routed to the US, Europe, Asia and Australia. We found no EU data residency option for Team or Enterprise.9
