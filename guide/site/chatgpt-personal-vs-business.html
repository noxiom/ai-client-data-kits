ChatGPT personal vs Business: what happens to your business data

ChatGPT's personal plans (Free, Plus, Pro) can train on what you type unless each person switches it off, and they give your firm no contract and no control. Business and Enterprise don't train on your data by default, come with a processor agreement and are managed by your admins. What matters most is which account someone is signed in to when they paste client data, not which plan the company pays for.

How to tell which one someone is using

  • A work account belongs to your company's Business or Enterprise workspace and shows up in the workspace's member list. If a colleague isn't on that list, any ChatGPT they use is a personal account.
  • Being on the member list doesn't settle it: one person can have a personal account and a work account at the same time. Ask which one was used for the work in question.
  • A personal account paid for by the employee, or expensed, is still a personal account. Expense claims for OpenAI are a quick way to find them.

What an admin should check
  • Sign OpenAI's Data Processing Addendum: OpenAI asks Business and Enterprise customers to complete its DPA form.
  • Choose the storage region when you create a Business workspace, if one is offered. OpenAI says an existing Business workspace can't switch region later.
  • Compare the member list with your staff list, invite people by their work address, and ask them to stop using personal accounts for client work.
  • Tell staff plainly what admins can and can't see. OpenAI's pages disagree for Business (see the questions below), so don't promise privacy you can't confirm.
  • Enterprise only: set a retention period and decide whether you need the Compliance API audit log.

Questions

Is it safe to put company data into ChatGPT?

It depends on the account, not on ChatGPT as such. In a personal account (Free, Plus or Pro), OpenAI may use what you type to train its models unless the person switches it off, and there is no contract between OpenAI and your firm. In ChatGPT Business or Enterprise, OpenAI says it doesn't train on your data by default, and it offers a Data Processing Addendum. Which company data may go in is then your firm's decision. No tool can honestly be called safe in general; this page shows what each account promises.1, 9

Is ChatGPT safe for confidential information?

Confidential information is more than personal data: client documents, prices, plans, anything under a non-disclosure agreement. The same account rule applies. A personal account gives your firm no contract and no control, and may use what is typed for training by default. A Business or Enterprise workspace doesn't train on it by default and comes with OpenAI's processing terms. Check your client contracts as well: some don't allow their information in any outside tool, whatever the plan.1, 4

Can my employer see my ChatGPT history?

Not in a personal account: your employer has no access to it. In a company workspace it depends on the plan. In Enterprise, admins can get an audit log of conversations through OpenAI's Compliance API. For Business, OpenAI's own pages disagree on whether admins can read members' chats, so assume they might.4, 10

Does ChatGPT Plus train on my data?

By default, yes. OpenAI may use content from Free, Plus and Pro to train its models unless you switch off "Improve the model for everyone" under Settings > Data controls. Temporary Chats are not used for training. If you rate a response, OpenAI may use that whole conversation even if you opted out.1, 2

Can admins see employees' chats in ChatGPT Business?

OpenAI's own pages disagree. Its enterprise privacy page says workspace admins can view, access, export and delete end-user conversations. Its Business FAQ says admins and owners cannot see all private member chats by default, and that self-service export isn't available in Business. Until OpenAI aligns the two, tell staff to assume an admin may be able to see work chats.4, 10

Can I use my personal ChatGPT account for work?

Technically yes; whether you may is your employer's decision. For client personal data it is a poor fit: your employer has no processor agreement with OpenAI for your personal account and no way to manage or delete what is in it. The Dutch data protection authority treats personal data entered into a chatbot against the employer's agreements as a data breach.4, 13

Where is ChatGPT Business data stored?

A new Business workspace can choose a storage region at checkout, where available; an existing workspace can't switch. OpenAI's page doesn't list the regions. Processing isn't tied to that region, and abuse-monitoring logs stay in the US. European data residency with processing in Europe is documented for Enterprise.7, 8
