About this kit

What it does and doesn't do

It gives you the questions clients ask, the rules behind them, and a statement and letter written only from what your firm confirmed. Vendor statements come from the AI accounts guide, checked {{guide_checked_human}}.

It doesn't check your setup, decide what your contracts allow, or replace advice. The Dutch wording of the statement and letter was written with AI: read it before you send it. It is not legal advice.
